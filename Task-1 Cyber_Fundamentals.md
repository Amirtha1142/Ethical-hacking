Core Principles of Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, applications, and data from cyberattacks, unauthorized access, damage, and theft.
Ethical hacking is an authorized process in which security professionals test systems to identify vulnerabilities and 
help organizations improve their security before malicious attackers exploit those weaknesses.

Hackers can be classified into different categories.
White hat hackers are authorized professionals who test and improve security. 
Black hat hackers perform illegal activities with malicious intentions, such as stealing data or damaging systems. 
Grey hat hackers may identify vulnerabilities without authorization, even if their intentions are not necessarily malicious. 
Other categories include script kiddies, who use existing hacking tools with limited technical knowledge, and hacktivists, 
who use cyber activities to promote particular social or political causes.

An important cybersecurity model is the CIA Triad, which consists of Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.
Confidentiality ensures that sensitive information is accessible only to authorized users.
Integrity ensures that information remains accurate and is not modified without authorization.
Availability ensures that systems and information are accessible to authorized users when required.

Common cybersecurity threats include malware, phishing, and ransomware.
Malware is malicious software that can steal information, damage systems, or provide unauthorized access.
Phishing is an attack that tricks users into revealing sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, or banking details.
Ransomware is a type of malware that blocks access to data or systems, often through encryption, and demands payment or another concession.

These threats can affect the CIA Triad. Malware may compromise confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
Phishing can lead to stolen credentials and unauthorized access or modification. 
Ransomware primarily affects availability by preventing users from accessing important files and systems, while some attacks may also involve data theft.

Conclusion:
Cybersecurity and ethical hacking are essential for protecting digital systems and information.
Understanding hacker types, the CIA Triad, and common threats provides a strong foundation for identifying risks and implementing appropriate security measures.
